# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **The mesh, owner and aggregate nodes take no `report` option.** Breaking: `MeshUpstreamOptions`, `OwnerMeshOptions`, the owner node's options, `createAggregate` and `startAggregateNode` lost `report`; what they told it is reported under `statenet.*` and as warnings through @punyts/core, which hosts listen to. Tests: `src/mesh/groups.test.ts`, `src/mesh/mesh.test.ts`, `src/mesh/network.test.ts` (now listen to the reporter).
- **Reporting by area, and `PUNYTS_REPORT`.** Breaking: the `statenet` category is replaced by `statenet.service` (namespace loads with ms, clients joining and leaving, refused connections and upgrades, emits relayed), `statenet.mesh` (links opened, closed and dropped from the directory, linking up, reconnects with their delay), `statenet.mesh.serving` (subscriptions served, slow nodes told to catch up, sessions, handoffs, saves and writes passed on with their outcome), `statenet.owner` (peer links, following a namespace's owner, sessions placed), `statenet.relay` (start, directory learned, links to other groups' relays), `statenet.relay.client` (browser subscriptions and their changes' outcomes), `statenet.aggregate` (saves with ms, folds and fences) and `statenet.certificate` (renewals asked, given and passed on). Refused links, refused saves and unanswered writes are `report.warning`, a failed save `report.error`, listening and stopping `report.info`. Both bins turn on `statenet.*`, `state-sync.*`, `info`, `warning` and `error` plus what `PUNYTS_REPORT` lists, and write `[statenet] <category> <message>` (or `[statenet-relay]`) to stderr; the relay bin no longer passes its own `upstream.report`. Fix: a namespace that fails to load reports the error's name, not its message. Tests: `src/hostReporting.test.ts`, `src/Statenet.test.ts` ("should report namespaces, clients and revisions …"), `src/mesh/aggregate.test.ts` ("should report the links, the relay and the save …").

- **Removing a namespace goes through the aggregate.** New mesh messages `remove` / `removed` (`RemoveOutcome`), `MeshUpstream.remove(address, epoch)` (asked again after a dropped link), `ServingOptions.remove`, and relays pass it up. `Aggregate.remove` runs after the saves of the namespace that came before it, refuses an epoch older than the newest (by its own count or the storage's), and answers `failed` when its storage has no `remove` (`AggregateStorageBackend`). `createAggregateStorage` has `remove`, and `createFileEpochStorage` removes a namespace's file and keeps its epoch. Tests: `src/mesh/aggregate.test.ts`, "removing a namespace".

- **Nodes link up to relays, in groups, and relays route between them** (docs/design/statenet-network.md, S9). A link now carries asks both ways: the serving of a link is `createServing` (`ServingOptions`, `Serving`, taken out of `startMeshServer`), the end that did not open the link asks through a `LinkClient` (`createLinkClient`, `MeshServer.peer(nodeId)`) with its messages wrapped as `rev`, and a node that dialled serves them with `MeshUpstreamOptions.serve`. A node's certificate names its `group` (`NodeCertificateClaims.group`, `MeshServerOptions.group`, `MeshUpstreamOptions.group`), and a node refuses a node of another group at either end. `subscribe`, `write`, `open`, `claim` and `take` may name the node they are for (`to`; `from` on `write` and `claim`), `MeshUpstream`'s methods take them, `reachThrough(link, nodeId)` binds them, and `startRelayNode` routes such an ask down the node's link or to the relay the directory says it is under (`DirectoryView.nodes[].group` and `parent`, `ServingOptions.route`, `RouteTarget`). `startOwnerMesh` takes `group` and `relays` and has `uplink`. Breaking: `PeerLinks.link` answers a `RouteTarget`, not a `MeshUpstream`. Tests: `src/mesh/groups.test.ts`.

- **The aggregate: the only writer of state** (docs/design/statenet-network.md, S8). `createAggregate(storage)` saves the namespaces nodes send it through an `EpochStorage`, one save of a namespace at a time: saves that arrive meanwhile are folded into the newest, a save sent again writes nothing, and a save under an older epoch is answered `fenced`. `createAggregateStorage(link)` is the storage of a node that keeps nothing itself: its save is done when the aggregate says so, and a fence throws `FencedError`. New mesh messages `persist` / `persisted` and `load` / `loaded` (`PersistOutcome`, `LoadOutcome`), `MeshUpstream.persist` and `load` (sent again after a dropped link), `MeshServerOptions.persist` and `load`; a relay passes them up, and `startAggregateNode` runs the mesh server of role `aggregate`. `createFileEpochStorage` keeps namespaces and their epochs in files. Needs state-sync's `FencedError` with its fields. Tests: `src/mesh/aggregate.test.ts`.

- **Mesh protocol 2: roles, sealed links, listed nodes and certificate renewal** (docs/design/statenet-network.md, S7). Breaking: a node of protocol 1 is refused, so every node of a statenet is upgraded together. `mayLink` says which role may dial which, and both ends of a link refuse the rest; a hello must name its certificate's role. The handshake agrees a key (`newShare`, `sealOf`, `transcript`: X25519, each share signed in the proofs) and every message after `link-proof` is sealed with AES-256-GCM and a counter per direction; a message in the clear, changed, replayed or out of order closes the link. The directory moved from `link-welcome` (now carrying `share`) to the first sealed frame. `MeshServerOptions.requireListed` asks that nodes of the named roles be in the directory with their key (`DirectoryView.nodes[].publicKey`), `reread` reads it again for a newcomer, and `directoryChanged` closes the link of a node that left it. A node asks its parent for its next certificate before the old one ends (`certify`, `renewWithinMs`, `onCertificate`, `MeshUpstream.certificate()` and `certify()`; `renewNodeCertificate` for the authority; relays pass the ask up), and `statenet-relay` writes it over its certificate file. `certificate` may be a function. Tests: `src/mesh/network.test.ts`.

- **Relays from Magnetic's files.** `statenet-relay` reads the authority key from a file (`--authority-key-file`, `STATENET_AUTHORITY_PUBLIC_KEY_FILE`), as `magnetic relay-certificate` writes it, and a mesh upstream reports each link it opens.
- **Owner nodes over the mesh.** `startOwnerMesh` runs an owner node of an app server with its links: `createPeerLinks` links to the other owner nodes the directory names (forwarded writes, claims and handoffs, and a peer asked to claim, as `claim`, `handoff`, `handed`, `take` and `taken` messages), `connect` serves a client session with the authority here or tunnels it to the owner (`open`, `say`, `shut`), so a view keeps its writes and emits both ways, and `createPlacedUpstream` keeps a relay subscription following a namespace as it moves. A mesh server takes `sessions`, `handoff` and `take`, and passes the node a write came from to `write`; a mesh upstream with `followDirectory: false` stays on its parents. Tests: `ownerMesh.test.ts`.

- **The mesh: links between nodes, and relay processes.** `startMeshServer` is a node's side of the links below it: one
  WebSocket per node, opened with a certificate Magnetic's authority signed (`issueNodeCertificate`) and a proof of the
  node's key each way, then frames of `subscribe`, `renew`, `changes`, `durable`, `write`, `revoked`, `credit` and
  `directory` messages. A subscription's changes cost a credit each; without credit they are folded, and past `maxHeld`
  the node below is told to catch up instead. `createMeshUpstream` is the side looking up: it learns the directory from
  its parent, reconnects to the next parent when a link goes silent, and subscribes again from each subscription's
  revision. `startRelayNode` (and the `statenet-relay` command) runs a relay with no database, serving relays below over
  the mesh and browsers over the sync protocol (`startClientServer`). Tests: `mesh.test.ts`.
- **Emit relay.** An `emit` from an app server (a client without `Origin`) is relayed to every other client of the
  namespace, an `emit` from a web page to the app servers only; nothing of it is stored. `@punyts/stateui-mcp` sends
  its view messages (documents, commands) this way and its pages their reports, app events and file requests.
- **Leaving.** When a client of a namespace disconnects, the others get the emit `statenet.left` with its `{ clientId }`
  (`LEFT_EMIT`).
- README: how stateui-mcp uses the statenet; the stale `$.sealed` relay description is gone.

## 0.1.0

- First version: `startStatenet` and the `statenet` CLI (`--port`, `--host`, `--data-dir`, `--log-length`,
  `--allow-origin`) run one `@punyts/state-sync` authority per namespace, with no server logic, behind a WebSocket at
  `/sync`. A namespace starts empty and creates missing parents.
- `createFileNamespaceStorage`: one folder per namespace with `namespace.json`, written atomically, one writer at a
  time per namespace; temporary files a crash left are removed on load.
- Listens on loopback only; refuses a non-loopback `Host` and browser origins that are not allowed.
- README: how an app server relays to the statenet (`$.server` as `$.sealed`) and how Persisted pages are admitted.
- Logs through the `@punyts/core` reporter, never change values.
